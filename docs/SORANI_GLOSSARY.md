Keep intentional product names, BMR/TDEE abbreviations and numeric quantities readable. Phone numbers and OTP inputs remain logically LTR inside RTL Sorani forms. Do not mirror graphs or reverse numeric values.

Before release, obtain native Sorani review of regional corn/peanut and other food terms, thyroid/gout/medical labels, long health disclosures, AI-generated recipes/guidance and stock system dialogs. Automated coverage verifies presence/placeholders, not linguistic accuracy.
